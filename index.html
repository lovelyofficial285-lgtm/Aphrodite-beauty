About

Welcome to Aphrodite Beauty.

Aphrodite Beauty specialises in microblading, skin rejuvenation and beautiful lash treatments. From natural-looking lash extensions to lash lifts and rejuvenating skin care, every appointment is tailored to help you feel confident and beautifully refreshed.

Our welcoming team takes great care to create a relaxing experience, with attention to detail, quality products and a comfortable treatment room. Whether you are new to lashes or a regular client, you will always be made to feel at ease.
